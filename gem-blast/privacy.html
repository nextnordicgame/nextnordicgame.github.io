Gem Blast: Charmers — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Gem Blast: Charmers (Android, package com.gemcharmers.blast) collects, why, and your choices. The game is published by NextNordicGame ("we"). Contact: nextnordicgame@gmail.com.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, or create an account for you.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • We use Google services for leaderboards, analytics, crash reports and ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before personalised ads and analytics.
  • The game contains no real-money gambling. Casino themes are decorative only.

Legal bases (GDPR): consent for analytics and personalised advertising where required; legitimate interests for crash reporting, security and non-personalised ads; performance of the service for leaderboards you choose to use.

Your choices

  • Consent: You can change your consent at any time via Settings → Privacy options in the game.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Play Games: You can sign out or delete Play Games data in the Google Play Games app.
  • Delete game data: Uninstalling the game removes local progress. To request deletion of analytics data linked to your app instance, email us with the subject "Data deletion"; we will process it within 30 days.

Retention

Analytics event data is retained for 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days; local game data stays on your device until you uninstall.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
